Play Reference · four directions

Presence without a drop shadow

design-rules.md rules out obvious drop shadows and hover lift — the current button uses both. Each direction below finds its weight somewhere else. Hover and press them.

Current being replaced

A block floating 3px off the page. Reads as a game button, not a printed one.

A · Flat Ink colour is the only signal

Strip everything. The burnt orange is already the loudest thing on a paper-white page — let mass do the work.

Trade-off — the quietest press. Nothing moves, so it can feel less physical than the old one.
B · Pressed Stamp the press goes inward

Keeps the tactility the old one was reaching for, but the depth pushes into the paper instead of hovering above it.

Trade-off — the ink hairline slightly mutes the orange, and it is still a shadow, just an honest one.
C · Underscored a printed rule answers you

The page already speaks in hairlines. Give the button its own rule, and let that rule extend past the edges when you reach for it.

Trade-off — the extending rule is the most "designed" gesture here; it may read as fussy next to plainer controls.
D · Hairline Ghost fills as you approach

Outlined at rest, solid on hover. The calmest page of the four — the orange arrives only when you mean to act.

Trade-off — under-signals. design-rules.md asks the primary action to be solid orange; this only becomes solid on hover, and never on touch.
